Payment Settings

Connect the payment provider for this AgentCoPilot deployment. Customer payments will be processed through the merchant account you connect.

Connect a payment provider

Choose Paystack or Flutterwave. Start in test mode, verify the integration, and switch to live mode only after your merchant account is approved.

Your secret key and webhook secret are encrypted on the server. They are never sent back to this page. Never paste your bank password, account PIN, or provider dashboard password here.

For Paystack, use the webhook signing secret from Developers → API Configuration. For Flutterwave, use the webhook secret hash configured in your Flutterwave dashboard.

Viewing fee and booking deposit

Set the charges customers see when requesting a viewing or booking a property. These settings apply to AgentCoPilot-managed listings only. External listings are not payable through your account.

For percentage deposits, enter a number from 0.01 to 100. Fixed fees and deposits use the selected currency. Listings in a different currency must not be charged until a matching-currency policy is configured; no automatic currency conversion is assumed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Select a question to open its answer. Select it again to close the answer.

How do I create an AgentCoPilot account?

Choose Create Account, enter your name and email, create a password, select your country, and submit the registration form. Use the same email and password when you sign in.

Where can I see my profile details?

Select the circular initials icon beside the notification bell, or choose My Profile from the dashboard navigation. Your profile shows the name, email, country, account type, and membership date saved for your signed-in account.

Can I use someone else’s account or see their private profile?

No. Your profile page displays the information associated with your own signed-in account. Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared device.

How do I find properties?

Open Property Marketplace from the navigation. Browse the available listings and use the available search and filter controls to narrow your results.

How do I publish a property?

Sign in, open Property Marketplace, complete the property listing form with accurate details, and submit it. Your listing is subject to the platform’s publication and review rules.

How do I manage my property listings?

Open Property Marketplace while signed in as the seller. Your own listings appear in the seller listings section, where available actions depend on each listing’s status.

How do viewing fees and booking deposits work?

Where a seller or the platform has enabled a viewing fee or deposit, follow the payment instructions shown for that property. Check the amount and payment mode before confirming.

Where can I find my payment history or receipt?

Open the dashboard and review the payment history area when available. Payment confirmations and receipt details may also appear in your notifications.

Why does a payment show test mode?

A test-mode payment is a simulated transaction for checking the payment flow. It is not a real payment and does not mean money has been transferred.

How do I contact a seller about a property?

Use the enquiry or contact option shown on the property listing. Provide clear contact details and a message about the property you are interested in.

How do I mark a property as sold?

If you are the seller of a published sale listing, open your seller listings in Property Marketplace and choose Mark as sold. Confirm the action. A sold listing is removed from public availability.

What should I do if I cannot sign in?

Check that you are using the email address and password registered to your account. If the issue continues, use the password recovery option on the sign-in page if available.

How do I keep my account secure?

Use a unique password, do not share verification codes or passwords, sign out on shared devices, and contact platform support if you notice activity you do not recognize.

Owner Control Centre

Owner authority is enforced by the server. Administrators cannot access this control or change the owner's authority.

Administrator access

Enter the administrator's Firebase Authentication UID. Pausing removes their administrator privileges; restoring returns their existing admin role. It does not delete their account or data.

Where to find the UID

Open Firebase Console → Authentication → Users. Select the administrator account and copy its User UID. Do not use the email address in the UID field.
